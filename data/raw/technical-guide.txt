## Switching the sensor on

PROXI GEN
CMU1

###### POWER ON

#### Hold for 3 seconds

1Find the power button on the
side of the unit.

2Press and hold it for 3 seconds.

3Release when the LED gives one
long flash. The sensor is now on
and logging.

###### LED SIGNAL

**One long flash**

The sensor has switched on.

###### Before installation

Switch the sensor on and add it in the Proxigen app (pages 08–09) before you fix it in place. You will need a
smartphone with Bluetooth and Location switched on.

###### Good to know

Once switched on, the sensor logs every 15 minutes and runs up to 6 months on its battery.

## Cast into the concrete

Fix the unit directly to the reinforcement so it is cast into the pour. You need two zip ties for the unit and
one small zip tie for the probe cable.

PROXI GEN

###### Select the location

PROXI.GEN

Choose a clean, accessible rebar close to
the top of the element, where you want to
measure.

**Surface max. 7.6 cm (3 in)**

###### 2 Zip-tie the unit to the rebar

Hold the unit beside the rebar. Thread a zip
tie through the slot in one mounting tab,
wrap it around the rebar and tighten firmly.
Use a second zip tie on the opposite tab.

###### Position the probe

3 Position the probe

Run the probe straight without pressing on
the unit. Secure the cable gently to the rebar
with a small zip tie so it is stable but not
strained.

###### Maximum depth 7.6 cm (3 in)

The unit must not be buried more than 7.6 cm (3 in) below the concrete surface, or the wireless connection
to your phone may be lost.

## Formwork mounting and probe placement

PROXI GEN
CTM.01

###### OPTION B

##### Mount on the formwork

Fix the unit to the outside of the formwork with a
screw through each mounting tab.

2Run the probe cable into the pour and fix the
probe at the measuring point.

3Keep the cable clear of moving parts and of the
formwork joint.

PROXI GEN

###### CTM02 ONLY

##### Placing two probes

Fix one probe near the top of the
element and the other near the
bottom.

2Route both cables from the Y-splitter
without pulling or kinking them.

3The app then shows both readings, so
you can follow the temperature
difference across the element.

###### Then pour as normal

Keep the probes straight while pouring. The sensor logs every 15 minutes; open the app near the unit to
sync the data.

###### TROUBLESHOOTING

###### The app does not find the sensor

Check that Bluetooth and Location are on, stay within 5–10 m of the unit
and make sure the sensor is switched on (hold the button 3 s; the LED
gives one long flash).

###### The connection drops

Move closer to the unit. If it is cast in, check it is no deeper than 7.6 cm
(3 in) below the surface.

###### No new readings

Open the app near the unit and connect to sync. The sensor logs every
15 minutes.
