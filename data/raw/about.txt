Our story

## Built to end the guesswork in concrete

Proxigen was founded with a clear purpose: to solve one of the most persistent challenges in construction, understanding concrete behavior in real time.

For decades, the construction industry relied on manual cube testing, delayed laboratory results, and assumptions that often led to project delays, rework, and disputes. Our founders, with backgrounds in embedded systems, IoT, and industrial monitoring, recognized the need for a smarter, more reliable approach.

Through continuous prototyping, field testing, and collaboration with construction professionals, Proxigen developed the CTM01 Concrete Monitoring System: a compact, wireless device that delivers accurate, real-time data from inside concrete structures. Paired with an intuitive mobile application and cloud platform, CTM01 transforms raw measurements into meaningful, actionable insights.
