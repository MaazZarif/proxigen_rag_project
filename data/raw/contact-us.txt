Who we are

## The team behind Proxigen

Proxigen is a technology company focused on one thing: smart concrete monitoring. We design the sensors, the wireless link, the mobile app and the cloud dashboard ourselves, so construction teams get real-time insight into concrete temperature, strength development and curing from a single partner.

### Built for concrete

Our solution is designed only for concrete monitoring: temperature, strength development and curing behaviour on real construction sites.

### End-to-end technology

Hardware, wireless connectivity, mobile app and cloud dashboard from one team, with no extra vendors or disconnected tools.

### Made for the job site

Compact, durable sensors that are quick to install and keep recording in harsh site conditions.
