The maturity method

## Concrete tells you how strong it is. _Through heat._

01Pour02Heat03Strength04Ready

**01** Pour0 hProbes go in _before the concrete._Tie the probe to the rebar at the point you care about, usually the core of the section. Switch the sensor on and it records a reading every 15 minutes.

**02** Heatfirst 1 to 2 daysHydration _heats the core._Cement reacts with water and releases heat, usually peaking within the first day. With CTM02 you see the top and the bottom of the pour side by side.

**03** Strengthday 2 to 4Heat × time = _maturity._The maturity method turns the temperature history into an estimate of in-place strength, so you depend less on waiting for cube results.

**04** Readyday 4+Strip on data, _not the calendar._When the estimated strength reaches your target, plan formwork removal with the full temperature record to back the decision.

## Same Setup for Both Sensors

No pairing menus, no settings to change.

01

### Hold for 3 seconds

Press and hold the button. One long flash confirms the device is on.

02

### Scan the QR code

Open the Proxigen app and scan the QR code on the device.

03

### See the first reading

The app connects on its own and shows the first temperature.

## No Reading Gets Lost

Both sensors record a reading every 15 minutes and keep about 6 months of data in their own memory. When the app connects, every stored reading is sent to Proxigen Cloud, so gaps between site visits never become gaps in your records.
