## Concrete monitoring: common questions

### What is a concrete monitoring system?

A concrete monitoring system uses sensors placed in the concrete to record its temperature while it cures. The Proxigen app and cloud turn those readings into maturity and estimated in-place strength, so your team can see how each pour is developing without waiting for cube test results.

### Do you supply concrete sensors in Saudi Arabia, the UAE and Qatar?

Yes. Proxigen supports construction projects across Saudi Arabia, the United Arab Emirates, Qatar, Bahrain, Kuwait and Oman from our office in Dubai. Contact us for a quote for your project.

### How does concrete maturity monitoring help in hot weather?

High temperatures speed up early strength gain and raise the risk of thermal cracking, especially in large pours. Live internal temperature readings let you follow curing and temperature differences in the concrete instead of relying on assumptions.

### What is the difference between CTM01 and CTM02?

CTM01 has one probe (30 cm or 3 m) plus a built-in body sensor and suits everyday pours such as slabs, columns, walls and precast elements. CTM02 has two probes (2 m and 3 m) to measure the top and bottom of thick pours and the temperature difference between them.

### How do readings get from the sensor to the cloud?

Each sensor takes a reading every 15 minutes and stores about 6 months of data on the device. When the Proxigen app connects over 2.4 GHz RF, every stored reading is sent to Proxigen Cloud, so gaps between site visits never become gaps in your records.

### Which industries use Proxigen concrete sensors?

Construction and infrastructure, industrial construction and manufacturing, precast and ready-mix operations, warehousing and commercial facilities, and energy and utilities infrastructure.
