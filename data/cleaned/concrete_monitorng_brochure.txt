# Choose Your Model

## CTM01 — One Probe
- One temperature probe in the concrete.
- Probe cable 30 cm or 3 m, or custom length.
- Built-in body sensor.

## CTM02 — Two Probes
- One cable that splits into two probes.
- Top and bottom probes track the temperature differential.
- Probe lengths 2 m + 3 m, or custom length.
- Built-in body sensor.

# From the Pour to Your Phone

Our sensors track the temperature of curing concrete and turn it into maturity data, helping you plan formwork stripping, loading and the next pour using real curing data instead of fixed waiting times.

### Installation
Zip-tie the IP67 unit to the rebar and cast it in, no deeper than 7.6 cm (3 in), or mount it outside on the formwork with the probe in the pour.

### Data Logging and Connectivity
- Logs readings every 15 minutes.
- Battery life of up to 6 months.
- Syncs to the Proxigen app over a 2.4 GHz RF link.

### Live Monitoring and Reports
Follow curing in the Proxigen mobile app and Cloud dashboard. Download Excel or PDF reports.

# Setup in 4 Steps

### 1. Power On
Hold the power button for 3 seconds until the LED flashes.

### 2. Connect in the App
Tap **Add Sensor** in the Proxigen app and scan the QR code.

### 3. Install the Sensor
Zip-tie it to the rebar, no deeper than 7.6 cm (3 in), or mount it on the formwork with the probe in the pour.

### 4. Pour and Monitor
Pour as normal and monitor curing, with readings logged every 15 minutes.

For full setup and technical details, refer to the Proxigen Setup & Technical Guide.
