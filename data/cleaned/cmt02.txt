# CTM02 Dual-Probe Concrete Logger

Two probes, one device. Place one near the top of the pour and one near the bottom, and monitor the temperature difference across the section from your phone. Readings are recorded every 15 minutes.

## Top, Bottom, and the Difference

In thick pours, the core and the surface cure at different temperatures. CTM02 measures both so you can see the temperature difference.

### Top probe

Placed near the surface, where the concrete loses heat fastest.

### Bottom probe

Placed deeper in the section, where heat builds up during curing.

### Body sensor

Built into the housing and measures the temperature at the logger itself.

### Temperature difference

With both probes reporting, you can monitor the temperature difference between the top and bottom that matters for thermal cracking in thick sections.

## The Concrete Maturity Method

### Step 1: Pour

Place the probes before the concrete is poured. Tie the probes to the rebar at the desired measurement points, usually the core of the section. Switch on the sensor. It records a reading every 15 minutes.

### Step 2: Heat

Cement reacts with water and releases heat, usually peaking within the first day. With CTM02, you can see the temperature readings from the top and bottom of the pour side by side.

### Step 3: Strength

The maturity method uses the temperature history to estimate in-place concrete strength, reducing reliance on waiting for cube test results alone.

### Step 4: Readiness

When estimated strength reaches the project's target, the temperature record can help support formwork-removal planning. Follow the project's approved engineering criteria and procedures.

## Setup: On and Connected in Under a Minute

One button and one scan. No pairing menus or settings to change.

### Step 1: Switch on

Press and hold the button for 3 seconds. One long flash of the LED confirms the device is on.

### Step 2: Scan the QR code

Open the Proxigen app and scan the QR code on the device.

### Step 3: See the first reading

The app connects automatically and shows the first temperature from the body and both probes.

## No Reading Gets Lost

CTM02 records temperature every 15 minutes and stores readings in its own memory, with capacity for about 6 months of readings. When the app connects, stored readings are transferred and saved in Proxigen Cloud.

If you miss a site visit, the data remains on the device until you reconnect.

- **Reading interval:** Every 15 minutes
- **On-device storage:** About 6 months of readings
- **Data synchronization:** Stored readings upload when the app connects

## Technical Specifications

| Specification | Details |
|---|---|
| Number of probes | 2 |
| Probe lengths | 2 m and 3 m as standard; custom lengths on request |
| Sensors | Two probe sensors and one built-in body sensor |
| Differential measurement | Temperature difference between the two probes |
| Wireless | 2.4 GHz RF |
| Connection | Proxigen app on iOS and Android, with data synced to Proxigen Cloud |
| Reading interval | Every 15 minutes |
| On-device storage | About 6 months of readings; storage outlasts the battery |
| Battery | 3.0 V |
| Indicator | Single-colour LED; one long flash when switched on |
| Power | Press and hold the button for 3 seconds |
| Setup | Scan the device's QR code in the Proxigen app |
