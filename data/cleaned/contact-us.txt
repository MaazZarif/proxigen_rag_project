## About the Proxigen team

Proxigen is a technology company focused on smart concrete monitoring. It develops sensors, wireless connectivity, a mobile app, and a cloud dashboard to provide construction teams with real-time insight into concrete temperature, strength development, and curing.

### Built for concrete

The solution focuses on monitoring concrete temperature, strength development, and curing behavior on construction sites.

### End-to-end technology

Hardware, wireless connectivity, a mobile app, and a cloud dashboard are provided as an integrated solution.

### Made for the job site

The company provides compact, durable sensors designed for quick installation and recording in demanding site conditions.
