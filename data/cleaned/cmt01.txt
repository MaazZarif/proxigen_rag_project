# CTM01 Concrete Temperature Logger

One probe in the concrete and one sensor in the body, recording every 15 minutes from the pour to the day you strip the forms. Switch on, scan, and the first temperature appears in the Proxigen app.

- Probes: 1
- Probe length: 30 cm or 3 m
- Wireless: 2.4 GHz RF
- On-device storage: Approximately 6 months

## Two Temperatures from One Device

CTM01 reads the concrete and the logger at the same time, and the app shows each one clearly.

### Probe Sensor

Sits inside the pour at the depth you choose and measures the concrete temperature that drives strength gain.

### Body Sensor

Built into the device housing and measures the temperature at the logger itself, outside the concrete.

## The Concrete Maturity Method

### Step 1: Pour

Probes go in before the concrete. Tie the probe to the rebar at the point you care about, usually the core of the section. Switch the sensor on and it records a reading every 15 minutes.

### Step 2: Heat

Cement reacts with water and releases heat, usually peaking within the first day. With CTM02, you see the top and the bottom of the pour side by side.

### Step 3: Strength

The maturity method turns the temperature history into an estimate of in-place strength, so you depend less on waiting for cube results.

### Step 4: Ready

When the estimated strength reaches your target, plan formwork removal with the full temperature record to back the decision.

Illustrative curing curve. Real values depend on your mix and site conditions.

## Setup

One button, one scan. No pairing menus or settings to change.

### Step 1: Switch On

Press and hold the button for 3 seconds. One long flash of the LED confirms the device is on.

### Step 2: Scan the QR Code

Open the Proxigen app and scan the QR code on the device.

### Step 3: See the First Reading

The app connects automatically and shows the first temperature from the body and the probe.

## No Reading Gets Lost

CTM01 records the temperature every 15 minutes and keeps it in its own memory, with room for about 6 months of readings. When the app connects, every stored reading is sent across and saved in Proxigen Cloud.

Missed a site visit? The data waits on the device until you are back.

- Reading interval: Every 15 minutes
- On-device storage: About 6 months of readings
- Data synchronization: All stored readings upload when the app connects
